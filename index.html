Questions people ask before they order

Is the pattern printed on?

No. It is a jacquard knit, so the cream wave is part of the fabric structure. There is no printed layer to peel, crack, or fade where your hand rubs the bag.

Is it waterproof?

No, and we would rather say so up front. A light drizzle is fine and the knit sheds most of it, but a real downpour will get through. There is no coating on this fabric.

Will a 13-inch laptop fit?

The bag is 16.5 inches wide at the top, so a slim 13-inch laptop slides in flat with room beside it for a bottle or a sweater. Bulky laptop sleeves eat most of that margin.

How do I clean it?

Spot clean with cool water and a little mild soap, then air dry flat. Skip the washing machine, the dryer, and bleach. A soft brush handles crumbs and lint.

What does shipping cost and how long does it take?

Shipping is free anywhere in the US. Orders leave the warehouse in 1–2 business days and usually arrive 3–6 days after that. You get a tracking number the day it ships.

Can I return it?

Yes, within 30 days of delivery, used or unused. Refunds go back to the original payment method within a few business days of the bag reaching us.
